= Website Notifications
:description: How to receive website push notifications through UnifiedPush in WebLibre.

WebLibre can show notifications from websites even when the browser is closed. Delivery uses UnifiedPush rather than Google's push service. You choose a separate delivery app (a *distributor*), such as ntfy, to carry those messages.

Website notifications are optional. A site cannot send them until you grant that site's permission, and delivery also depends on a distributor and Android's notification permission.

== Set Up Delivery

. Install a UnifiedPush distributor app if you do not already have one. Follow that app's setup instructions; some distributors use a service you choose or host yourself.
. Open **Settings > Notifications** in WebLibre.
. Under **UnifiedPush Distributor**, choose the installed delivery app.
. Under **Notification Permission**, grant Android's permission if it is not already granted.
. When a website asks to send notifications, choose whether to allow it.

The settings screen shows whether the distributor is ready and lists your **Site Subscriptions**. If a distributor is unavailable or Android permission is denied, notifications may not appear until you resolve that issue.

== Manage a Site

To stop one website from notifying you, open that site's information panel and change its **Notifications** permission to **Block** or **Ask**. The subscription list in **Settings > Notifications** shows sites using push, but it does not have a per-site unsubscribe button.

To disable website push delivery altogether, use **Disable web push** under **UnifiedPush Distributor**. Your distributor choice and subscriptions are separate for each WebLibre profile.

== What to Expect

* The website, the distributor app, and its delivery service are involved when you enable push. Choose a provider you trust; this feature does not make notifications anonymous.
* Messages can arrive while WebLibre is closed, but delivery depends on the distributor being available and on Android allowing notifications.
* Android's notification permission also controls other WebLibre notifications, such as download status. It does not grant permission to individual websites.

See xref:site-permissions.adoc[Site Permissions] to review other permissions a website can request.
